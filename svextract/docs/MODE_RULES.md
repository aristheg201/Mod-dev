# Current mode rules and outstanding integration

Versus targets Counter-Strike-style 5v5 defusal. The server owns round transitions, a buy phase, one life per round, planting, dropped-bomb ownership, interrupted defusing, detonation and match cash. Defaults: 15-second buy phase, 115-second round, 40-second bomb, 3-second plant, 10-second defuse or 5 seconds with a kit. Teams swap attacking/defending sides after round 12. First to 13 wins; 12–12 is a draw in the current regulation format. Overtime is not implemented.

`config/svextract/defusal.json` configures these rules. `defusal-shop.json` configures up to six real registry products and prices. Purchases use server match cash, not BEconomy balances. Permanent equipment is reserved and restored through the raid journal; match-issued equipment cannot be extracted into permanent ownership. B opens the configurable native buy-menu binding. Select the breach charge in slot 9 and use a site lever to plant. Use the planted lever as a defender to defuse; moving or taking damage interrupts the player interaction. Redline site A is (-10,65,20), B is (24,65,-20), relative to the instance origin.

The current world adapter is a **private one-human/four-friendly/five-hostile bot session**. A native short-rule test bought armor with server match cash, planted at A, detonated the bomb, swapped sides, won 2–0 and restored permanent equipment. Bomb timings/interruption, elimination, halftime and wallet behavior have automated tests. Runtime human defusing, default-length matches and real multiplayer acceptance remain outstanding. Bots do not yet buy equipment or retain their purchased loadouts; public matchmaking and the required two-minute autofill are not wired. This is not complete public Versus.

Battleground targets up to 60 combatants, solo or squads of four, with bot fill. Its latest crate requirement is interpreted as temporary match supplies: permanent equipment remains protected and match loot is not retained after the match. Extraction retains its separate gear-risk and persistent loot rules. Battleground rules, real players, safe-zone progression, supply-drop gameplay and settlement remain unimplemented; its model registration does not enable the mode.

Training and Operations remain private PvE. Public Black Zones, shared squad deployment, Apex and economy payouts are unfinished. Unsupported modes remain unavailable until their server engines exist. The native lobby must not advertise an unavailable engine as playable.
