# Voice reference — 0.3.2

Vietnamese is the primary authored language. Ordinary lines are short, specific and grounded in the speaker's current knowledge. A question branch adds information; acceptance gets an acknowledgment; refusal ends the conversation without completing the task. An offer becomes an active side quest only on final acceptance.
