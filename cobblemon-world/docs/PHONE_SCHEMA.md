# Trainer Phone persistence schema

Persistent fields:

- level cap
- current story
- current objective
- story flags
- badges
- contacts
- unread messages
- active side quests
- completed side quests

Phone apps:

1. Trainer Card
2. Objective
3. Current Story
4. Side Quests
5. Level Cap
6. Badges
7. Contacts
8. Messages
9. League
10. Faction

Notifications render as Minecraft/Cobblemon-style top-right toasts. Full conversations remain in Messages.
